## Findings and smallest decisions

### Correctness: accounting agrees; preserve both fixtures

All three separate external flows from profit, charge trade fees once through
cash, and value holdings with an available mark rather than a future price.
RISK-001 starts at zero and deposits 1,000; RES-001 starts with 100 already in the
account. These are fixture variations, not contradictory profit definitions.
Use `profit = ending equity - opening equity - interval net external flows`.
Risk's abbreviated formula works because its opening equity is zero.

**Decision proposed:** allow an explicit validated opening snapshot. Record opening
equity and begin interval flow/fee counters at zero. Opening capital is not also an
interval deposit. For the first fixtures, opening holdings are zero; do not expand
initialization to cost-basis or historical profit reconstruction. The opening
baseline can be supplied by the caller; no accounting architecture change is needed.

### Interface: representation differs from display, not economic units

Engineering proposes whole asset units and signed 64-bit minor currency units;
risk displays currency units and exact cents. They are compatible.

**Decision proposed:** one fictional currency with 100 minor units per currency
unit. Money and per-unit prices enter the core as integer minor units; holdings
are whole asset units. Thus a price of 12 becomes 1,200, and risk's final equity is
108,200 minor units. Reject overflow before changing any state. Keep parsing and
malformed textual-number checks at an input boundary; the typed core checks its
numeric ranges. No fractional rounding convention is needed yet.

### Correctness: execution price and mark must remain separate

Risk sells five units at 11 while the mark remains 12. That reduces equity by 5
relative to the previous mark, plus the 1 fee. It is compatible with research's
sale at the mark; it adds a useful check that research's fixture cannot expose.

**Decision proposed:** trades never update the mark. With zero holdings, equity is
cash even if no mark exists. With positive holdings and no available mark, equity
is unavailable; profit is also unavailable if either endpoint equity is missing.
Accept a cash-valid trade without a mark, but do not manufacture a valuation.
For the bounded first contract, require positive integer mark prices; revisit
zero-price assets explicitly if later scope requires them.

### Correctness: apply the no-borrowing rule to every event

Engineering explicitly rejects unaffordable buys and withdrawals but does not
explicitly mention a sale whose fee exceeds cash plus proceeds. Risk's insufficient
cash check can cover it, but both should reference one invariant.

**Decision proposed:** every accepted event leaves cash and holdings nonnegative.
Example rejection: cash 0, one unit sold at 1, fee 2 would leave cash -1.
Reject atomically, preserving balances, counters, mark, and accepted sequence.
This is a contract clarification required before implementation, not a detected
software bug. Separate deposit/withdrawal types require strictly positive amounts.

### Interface: choose one ordering rule

Unique event IDs in risk and increasing sequence numbers in engineering need not
create two identifier systems. Use a strictly increasing sequence number as the
unique event ID for the initial fixture stream; gaps are allowed. If timestamps
are included, reject decreasing timestamps and use sequence to order equal times.
An event uses only the mark already available in that stream. A rejected event
does not consume an accepted sequence number. Prefix checks then have a precise
meaning: later events cannot change previously reported values.

### Maintainability and preference

No maintainability blocker found in these document proposals. Keep a single agreed
contract so tests and explanations use the same conventions. Risk suggests a
possible balance-update apprentice contribution; engineering/research suggest
valuation. This is a planning preference, not an accounting conflict. Retain
engineering's reserved valuation contribution pending discussion; issue no
assignment from this review.
