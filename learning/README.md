# Working apprenticeship

## Goal

Become a teammate whose work other people can understand, verify, and rely on, with increasing responsibility across engineering, research, and risk.

The apprentice knows some C++ and is new to the domain. There is no assumed quant knowledge, fixed learning speed, or promise of employment equivalence. Use observed work to set support levels.

## Assignment template

- ID, owner, mentor, reviewer, and state
- Business question and why this contribution is needed
- Current reasoning, alternatives, and uncertainties
- Vocabulary and focused prerequisite explanations/references
- Inputs, outputs, units, interfaces, and components to reuse
- What is outside this component's responsibility
- Acceptance criteria and review method
- Dependencies and independent work the team can continue
- First step: apprentice restatement and proposed approach
- Submission location and handoff requirements

## Feedback record

Record concrete evidence of strengths and next growth areas: clarifying requirements, domain understanding, correctness, reproducibility, communication, revisions, and handoff. Do not assign invented numerical grades or infer skill from an agent-written solution.
